Our Story

A working farm in the heart of Mato Grosso

Araujo Farms is the home of Fazenda Nova Campinas, a 1,340-hectare property in the municipality of Tesouro, in Brazil's Mato Grosso state. It is cattle country: rolling pasture, fertile clay soils, and long frontage on the clear-running Rio Garças.

The farm balances production and preservation. Alongside its pasture and cultivated ground, more than a thousand hectares of native Cerrado remain standing — a legally protected reserve that keeps the springs flowing, the soil in place, and the wildlife at home.

Everything here is built for the long term: three-phase power, an artesian well, paved-highway access, and structures ready for a family and a working operation.

Raising Cattle

Pasture-raised, the Mato Grosso way

Cattle are at the centre of life here. The herd grazes across native and cultivated pasture, moving with the seasons and watered year-round by the Rio Garças and an artesian well that never runs dry.

Flat, open, fully fenced ground makes for calm, low-stress handling. The warm Cerrado climate and quality forage let animals put on condition on grass — the way ranching has always been done in this corner of Brazil.

The Land

Fertile ground, gentle country

Predominantly flat with soft undulations, free of erosion, and rich in clay — the kind of land that rewards good management.

🟫

Rich clay soils

Yellow and white clay, no sand, with clay content of 190–315 g/kg — medium-to-heavy soil that holds water and responds well to correction.

🗺️

Flat & mechanizable

Gently rolling and 100% mechanizable, with natural grottas and no sign of erosion anywhere on the property.

🌳

Preserved Cerrado

Over 1,047 ha of native vegetation kept standing as legal reserve — protecting water, soil and biodiversity.

The Rio Garças

Four kilometres of living water

The farm's back boundary follows the Rio Garças for four kilometres — clear water over rock and sand, framed by preserved gallery forest and quiet beaches.

It is the lifeblood of the property: water for the herd, a permanent-preservation corridor for wildlife, and a place to fish, cool off, and take a boat out at the end of a working day.

Infrastructure

Built to live and work on

🏡 Homestead & houses

A 110 m² main house, an 80 m² auxiliary house, and a 40 m² external suite, set among shade and fruit trees.

🏗️ Warehouse & storage

A 180 m² warehouse (5 m ceiling) with on-site lodging, plus a 25 m² store — room for machinery and feed.

⚡ Power & water

Three-phase electricity, a 5,000 L/h artesian well, and internet connectivity.

🚜 Paved access

3.5 km of frontage directly on the paved MT-110 highway — no dirt roads to reach the gate.

📑 Fully documented

Registered CAR, georeferenced title, and valid environmental licences on record.

🔒 Secured perimeter

The entire property is fenced, with a mix of pasture, cropland and preserved reserve.
